A home for businesses built to last.

Dubois & Co. is a holding company. We start new businesses and acquire established ones across many sectors, then hold them for the long term.

We don't flip companies. We look for good businesses with loyal customers and capable people, keep what makes them work, and give them the capital, patience and support to grow.

Our motto is on our logo: préserver ce qui fonctionne, faire croître le futur. Preserve what works. Grow what's next.

What we look for

Durable businesses, in any sector.

Proven economics

A track record of steady revenue and cash flow, with customers who come back.

Owners who care

Founders who want their legacy, team and customers looked after when they step back.

Room to grow

Clear opportunities to improve, expand or modernise without losing what works.
