04

Voice & boilerplate

Calm, plain and long-term. We sound like owners, not dealmakers.

  • Short sentences. Plain words. No jargon like “synergies” or “platform plays”.
  • Talk about people, customers and continuity before returns.
  • Confident, never boastful. No superlatives.
  • Sentence case everywhere. No exclamation marks.
  • Bilingual by default. Write French natively; don't translate word for word.
  • Our name is “Dubois & Co.” in running text, never “DuboisCo” or “Dubois and Company”.

Dubois & Co. builds and acquires durable businesses, and holds them for the long term.
